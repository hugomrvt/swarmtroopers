01 / The process

One page.
Many helping cursors.

Your content is already here.
Swarm Troopers coordinates its entrance.

01 — Assign

A clear part for every agent.

Swarm Troopers reads headings, cards, lists and dividers. Each section gets a squad with a shared queue of tasks.

02 — Act

A gesture you can follow.

Named agents work where you are looking. A caret, selection handles or a drawn line makes each action visible.

03 — Complete

The work lands. The team clears.

The last task finishes and the swarm signs off. Your original page stays ready to read, click and explore.
