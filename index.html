01 / why

Static pages get skimmed. Videos get muted. A page that builds itself gets watched.

Hand-made motion means a week of keyframes, redone every time the copy changes. Swarm Troopers reads the markup you already have and choreographs it for you, on every visit.

02 / how it works

one script tag. four beats.

No timeline to author. The agents read your page the way a reader does, top to bottom, section by section.

  1. 01

    Drop it in

    Add the script tag or npm i swarmtroopers. Point it at your main element.

    create({ root: 'main' })
  2. 02

    The orchestrator surveys

    It splits the page into sections and sends a squad to each one as it scrolls into view.

    orchestrator · 7 sections
  3. 03

    The squad builds

    Text is typed, boxes framed, lines drawn, lists stamped. Every gesture lands on your real content.

    ada · type() · h1
  4. 04

    Hand back

    The overlay goes idle and steps aside. What's left is your plain, untouched page.

    ✓ run complete

04 / the toolkit

four tools. zero keyframes.

Picked from your markup. Text gets typed, boxes get framed, lines get drawn, lists get stamped.

Made with careada type
type(el, text)

Words with a human rhythm

A click, a caret, bursts and pauses. Text appears where it already sits, so the layout never jumps.

mia frame
frame(el)

A precise drag for every box

Cards, images and fields open from one corner to the other behind a soft edge, then release.

noé rule
rule(el)

Lines drawn end to end

Separators and borders get one steady stroke.

iris stamp
stamp(list)

Lists pressed into place

The first item is framed. The rest are copied down and set in place, one stamp at a time.

09 / faq

fair questions.

Is there a model behind the agents?

No. No model, no server, no network request. The agents are choreography code running in the visitor's browser: movement shaped like a real hand, typing with human rhythm, all on your own content.

Does it hurt SEO or accessibility?

Your HTML is complete from the first byte. Search engines and screen readers read the whole page; the agents only change what is painted, never what is there.

Can I choose what gets built, and how?

Turn on auto and the agents pick a tool for each element. Or annotate: data-swarmtroopers="type", frame, rule, stamp, repeat, skip. The hero of this page is annotated by hand.

Won't visitors get impatient?

Sections are built as they come into view, never all at once. Anything left waiting too long simply appears, and anything scrolled past is shown straight away. Readers can also pick light or off, and the choice is remembered.

Does it work with my framework?

It works on any HTML. With React, Vue, Svelte or Astro, call create() when the view mounts and destroy() when it unmounts.

What does it cost?

Nothing. MIT licensed, free to use, open to fork.
