four tools

Picked from your markup. Text gets typed, boxes get framed, lines get drawn, lists get stamped.

Made with careada type
type(el, text)

Words with a human rhythm

A click, a caret, a short pause. Text appears where it already sits, so the layout never jumps.

mia frame
frame(el)

A precise drag for every box

Cards, images and fields open from one corner to the other, then release.

noé rule
rule(el)

Lines drawn end to end

Separators and borders get one steady stroke.

iris stamp
stamp(list)

Lists pressed into place

The first item is framed. The rest follow it, one stamp at a time.

guardrails

The agents are guests on your page. Four checks pass on every run.

install

one script tag.
no build step.

Keep your markup. Copy the script into your public folder, point it at your main element, and the agents do the rest.

<!-- Copy dist/swarmtroopers.core.iife.js into public/vendor. -->
<script
  src="/vendor/swarmtroopers.core.iife.js"
  data-swarmtroopers-init='{
    "root": "main",
    "auto": true,
    "controls": true
  }'
></script>
Serve the file from your own site. No build step needed.
