01 / About

Industrialize
without betraying.

I'm Chief Design Officer at Michelin Group and a member of the Digital Experience Executive Committee. My work is to orchestrate UX Transformation, design, content and digital experience across Michelin Group’s brands in 170 countries.

What matters most is not only what we build, but the quality of the experience people have with it. Every digital touchpoint has to help, clarify a choice, simplify a journey, create confidence, remove friction. At our scale, these details are not details, they are the experience.

That is the tension I hold every day: bringing the rigor of a century-old industrial company into the digital age. Between global ambition and local craft. Between long-term vision and daily execution. Between scale and soul.

This belief also shapes my commitments outside Michelin. I sit on the Board of APCI to contribute to national design policy, and on the Scientific Committee of ISCOM to help align future programs with industrial reality. Shaping the profession isn't separate from the work; it’s what allows that work to remain relevant ten years from now.
