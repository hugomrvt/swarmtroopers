Our server fetches the page once and caches it for ten minutes. Scripts run in an isolated sandbox: no forms, no logins, storage forgotten on reload. A page that tries to leave the sandbox is rebuilt without its own scripts. Want it on your own site? Install it: it runs on your HTML, no proxy.
